# FR-1.10, the model is kokoro v1.1-zh

| ID | Requirement | |
|---|---|---|
| FR-1.10 | Every clip is generated by kokoro's `hexgrad/Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh` model. | [A] |

I chose it for its three new English voices, af_maple, af_sol and bf_vale,
after hearing them, and said to switch everything to it. It is the
Chinese-focused release, so its English was measured against v1.0 first:
equally intelligible, the same speed, identical English phonemes. The older
voices run on it and sound different, higher for the two women measured and
quieter for all four; I heard that and accepted it.

The measurement is `.ephemera/kokoro-v11/REPORT.md` in a working checkout and
`clank/tasks/skid/voices/60` records it.
