# FR-4.11, every accepted submission is logged

| ID | Requirement | |
|---|---|---|
| FR-4.11 | Every submission skid accepts is logged when it is accepted: the time, the name, the work if it named one, and how many messages it carried. The messages themselves are never logged. | [A] |

I asked for logs after two days of hearing nothing, when skid could not say
whether anything had been sent. Its log recorded failures only, so a quiet
log meant both "everything worked" and "nobody called", and from the outside
those two look the same.

The text stays out. Agents speak about the work in front of them, and a log
that keeps every sentence becomes a second transcript nobody asked for. FR-8.3
already keeps substitutions out of the log for the same reason.
