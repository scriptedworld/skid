# FR-10.1, the voices available are configured

| ID | Requirement | |
|---|---|---|
| FR-10.1 | The voices skid may assign are listed in the config. A voice kokoro offers and the list does not name is never assigned. | [A] |

Asked for first-hand 2026-08-30: a filtered list of the voices our user prefers,
rather than every voice kokoro ships.

kokoro offers 54 and taste rules out most of them. The list is what makes
assignment unattended: skid picks without asking, so the set it picks from has
to have been agreed in advance.

**The list bounds assignment, not `set_voice`.** FR-6.1 and FR-6.2 reach one
setting and are how a person names a voice deliberately, which is a different
act from skid choosing one on a caller's behalf. A voice off the list stays
reachable by asking for it.

**It is also what keeps a silent voice out of the rotation.** Thirteen of the
54 cannot render on a machine without the Japanese and Chinese language packs,
and a list chosen by ear cannot contain one, because a voice that would not
render produced no sample to choose.
