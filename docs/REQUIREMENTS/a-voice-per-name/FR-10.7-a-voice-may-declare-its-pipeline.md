# FR-10.7, a voice may declare its pipeline

| ID | Requirement | |
|---|---|---|
| FR-10.7 | A configured voice may name the kokoro pipeline it is generated through. Where it does not, the pipeline is the one its own id implies. | [D] |

Derived from the shortlist I chose, which includes five voices that only work
this way.

A kokoro voice is a speaker embedding and a pipeline is a grapheme-to-phoneme
front end, and the two are separable. Taking the pipeline from the first letter
of the voice id welds them together, so `if_sara` means the Italian phonemiser
and there is no way to ask for Sara's timbre with English pronunciation.

A foreign timbre with English pronunciation is exactly what I kept. `ef_dora`, `em_alex`, `ff_siwis`,
`if_sara` and `im_nicola` are on the shortlist as English speakers with an
accent. Driven through their own pipelines they read English text through a
foreign phonemiser and come out mangled, which is a different thing and not what
was chosen.

The default keeps every other row honest. Twenty-three of the 28 say nothing
about a pipeline and get the pipeline their id implies, so this widens what can
be expressed without changing what an existing config means.

Pipeline codes are kokoro's to define and there are nine. They are not
enumerated in the schema, for the reason `voice` is not: `KPipeline` asserts
membership of its own set, and FR-6.5 catches a wrong one where the caller is
still present to be told.
