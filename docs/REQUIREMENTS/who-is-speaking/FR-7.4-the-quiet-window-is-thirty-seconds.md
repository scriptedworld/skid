# FR-7.4, the quiet window is thirty seconds

| ID | Requirement | |
|---|---|---|
| FR-7.4 | `x` in FR-3.2 is **30 seconds**, measured from the **end of the last clip spoken for that name**. | [A] |

Settled at the id the open question carried, and it gives FR-3.4 its
default.

Thirty seconds is short: a name that pauses between messages announces itself
again rather than being assumed to still hold the floor. That is what was asked
for.

Measured from the end of speech rather than from submission, because the whole
requirement is about what a listener knows, and the end of a clip is what a
listener experienced. Under FR-7.2's queue the two can differ by minutes, and
measuring from submission would announce a name whose voice was still audibly in
the room.
